## Rules

- Do not add an asset without recording its source and licence in `v2/docs/IMAGE_ASSET_REGISTER.md`.
- Use AVIF or WebP for production delivery.
- Use descriptive lowercase filenames.
- Keep the hero and CTA mobile-safe.
- Do not reference remote stock URLs from approved production pages.
- Do not add placeholder or irrelevant imagery merely to fill a slot.
