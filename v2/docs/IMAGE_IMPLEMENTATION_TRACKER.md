# TShirtSwiss Image Implementation Tracker

## Purpose

This tracker records the controlled replacement and QA of images across the TShirtSwiss website.

No row is complete until the image has been implemented and checked on desktop, tablet and mobile.

## Status key

- `Not started`
- `Current source recorded`
- `Candidate review`
- `Approved for implementation`
- `Implemented`
- `QA changes required`
- `Approved and locked`

## QA standards

### Desktop

- Hero subject remains clear behind text and overlays.
- No important details are cropped.
- Image is sharp at rendered size.
- Section image matches adjacent heading and copy.
- Gallery cards have consistent crop and height.

### Tablet

- Split layouts retain an informative crop.
- Faces and product details remain visible.
- Overlays do not make the image muddy.
- No stretching or unexpected aspect-ratio shift occurs.

### Mobile

- Main subject stays within the mobile safe area.
- Text remains readable.
- The crop still communicates the exact product, industry or service.
- Alternative mobile source is used where necessary.
- No hero depends on detail outside the viewport.

### Accessibility

- Informative images have accurate alt text.
- Decorative backgrounds are hidden from assistive technology where appropriate.
- Alt text does not repeat adjacent headings unnecessarily.
- Text contrast remains sufficient over background images.

### Performance

- Correct intrinsic dimensions are present.
- Responsive `srcset` or Elementor equivalent is configured.
- Above-the-fold hero loading is prioritised appropriately.
- Below-the-fold imagery uses lazy loading.
- AVIF/WebP delivery works.
- No oversized source file is delivered to small screens.
- Layout shift is prevented.

## Lock rule

Once a page is marked `Approved and locked`, do not change its imagery without:

1. Recording the reason.
2. Reopening the relevant tracker row.
3. Repeating responsive, accessibility and performance QA.
4. Recording the final decision in the project change log.
