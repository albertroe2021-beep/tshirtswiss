Hospitality buying guide

Choosing apparel for hospitality teams.

Hospitality buyers should consider more than colour and logo placement. The right garment depends on the venue style, staff roles, washing frequency, movement needs, climate, customer expectations and the way garments will be distributed across departments.

For front-of-house staff, presentation and fit may matter most. For kitchen and service teams, comfort, durability and easy care are often more important. For hotels and resorts, role-based uniforms can help guests identify staff while keeping the full team aligned visually.

  • Prioritise comfort
  • Match the venue style
  • Plan role-based garments
  • Use consistent branding
  • Plan replacement cycles
  • Keep reorders simple
