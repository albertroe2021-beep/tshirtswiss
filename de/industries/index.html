Planning by industry

The right production route depends on who will wear the garment and how it will be used.

Industry planning helps identify practical details before production begins. A healthcare uniform may prioritise cleanliness and repeat ordering. A franchise programme may need location packing. A retail collection may need labels and hang tags. An event range may need fixed-date delivery.

  • Product purpose
  • Wearer requirements
  • Branding method
  • Size and quantity planning
  • Packaging needs
  • Shipping destination

Industries FAQ

Can you support multiple industries?

Yes. We manufacture apparel for uniforms, retail, sports, healthcare, hospitality, trades, events and online brands.

Can you help choose the right product?

Yes. Share the use case, quantity, branding and delivery needs and we can recommend suitable product routes.

Can orders be packed by team or location?

Yes. Orders can be organised by branch, team, department, size, kit or destination when requirements are provided early.

Yes. Finished apparel can be prepared for worldwide shipping from Thailand.
