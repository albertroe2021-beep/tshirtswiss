Franchise apparel buying guide

Choosing apparel for multi-location franchise teams.

Franchise buyers should consider more than garment style. The right programme depends on the number of locations, staff roles, replacement cycle, rollout schedule, delivery route and how future orders will be managed. A system that works for five branches should still be practical at fifty branches.

For franchise operations, consistency is often as important as the garment itself. Clear brand standards, reliable sizing, approved colours and repeatable packing instructions can make apparel easier to manage as the network grows.

  • Define approved garment styles
  • Keep colour standards consistent
  • Plan location-level quantities
  • Use clear branding rules
  • Organise kits early
  • Keep reorders simple
