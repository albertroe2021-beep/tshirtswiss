Quote preparation

What to include in your quote request

A useful apparel quote is not just a price per piece. It should reflect the real garment, fabric, decoration, finishing, packing and shipping requirements. A generic price can become misleading if important details were not included.

For the fastest review, include garment type, estimated quantity per style, colour and size range, fabric preference, logo or artwork requirements, labels, packaging, sampling needs, delivery country and target deadline. If you have a tech pack, artwork, brand guidelines or previous sample, mention it in your request and email files directly to info@tshirtswiss.ch.

Quote review areas

These are the core factors that shape the production route and final price.

1

Garment type

T-shirts, polos, hoodies, uniforms, sportswear, workwear and private label apparel all follow different routes.

2

Branding method

Screen printing, embroidery, sublimation, heat transfer, woven labels and hang tags affect production planning.

3

Quantity and sizes

Quantities, size splits, colours and style variations help us estimate production and packing requirements.

4

Shipping route

Delivery country, deadline, carton organisation and packing instructions affect logistics planning.

Before you submit

A clear brief helps us quote faster and more accurately.

You do not need every detail finalised before contacting us, but the more information you provide, the better we can guide you. If you are unsure about fabric, decoration, sizing or packaging, explain the intended use and we can advise on practical options.

  • Product type and use case
  • Estimated quantity per style
  • Fabric or reference product
  • Artwork and logo positions
  • Labels, tags and packaging
  • Delivery country and deadline

Quote request FAQ

Can I request a quote without final specs?

Yes. Share the information you have and we can advise on what needs to be clarified before production.

Can I email files separately?

Yes. Tech packs, artwork, logo files, reference images and previous samples can be emailed to info@tshirtswiss.ch.

Do you quote private label projects?

Yes. Include labels, hang tags, packaging and any retail presentation requirements in your request.

Can you ship internationally?

Yes. Finished orders can be prepared for worldwide shipping from Thailand.
