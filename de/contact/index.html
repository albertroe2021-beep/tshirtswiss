Before you contact us

Helpful details for a faster production response.

The more detail you provide, the faster we can advise on the right production route. Even if your brief is not final, a few practical details help us understand the project.

  • Product type and quantity
  • Logo or artwork requirements
  • Fabric or reference garment
  • Size range and colours
  • Labels and packaging needs
  • Delivery country and deadline
