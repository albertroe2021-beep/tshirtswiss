Healthcare buying guide

Choosing apparel for healthcare teams.

Healthcare buyers should consider more than colour and logo placement. The right uniform depends on the role, workplace, washing frequency, movement needs, climate, patient-facing expectations and the way garments will be distributed across staff. A team with clinical, reception and support roles may need different garments that still feel part of one visual system.

For clinics and practices, professional presentation is often as important as durability. For care homes and high-use environments, comfort, easy movement and replacement planning may matter more. For private clinics and wellness teams, embroidery, premium fit and consistent colour can support a stronger brand image.

  • Prioritise staff comfort
  • Plan for repeated washing
  • Use clear colour systems
  • Confirm embroidery scale
  • Plan replacement cycles
  • Keep size reorders simple
