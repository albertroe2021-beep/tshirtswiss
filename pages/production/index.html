A structured production process for international apparel buyers.

Apparel production works best when every important decision is made before bulk manufacturing starts. Product type, fabric direction, sizing, decoration, labels, packaging, delivery country and quality requirements all affect the route. TShirtSwiss helps clients organise these details early so production can move with fewer surprises.

Our process is designed for companies, brands, teams, events and private label businesses that want production in Thailand with clear communication and Swiss-managed oversight.

The production workflow

A clear sequence helps keep communication, approvals and delivery aligned.

1
Brief

Share products, quantities, artwork, sizes and deadline.

2
Review

We check product route, branding and feasibility.

3
Plan

Specs, samples and requirements are defined.

4
Sample

Samples or references are reviewed when required.

5
Approve

Garments and branding details are signed off.

6
Produce

Bulk manufacturing and decoration begin.

7
QC

Goods, branding and packing are checked.

8
Ship

Finished goods are prepared for dispatch.

Planning stage

Production begins with a clear brief and realistic requirements.

Before production begins, we help clarify garment type, target quantity, product purpose, branding, delivery expectations and whether sampling is needed. This planning stage reduces uncertainty and helps the factory route match the commercial goal of the order.

  • Product and fabric direction
  • Quantity and size planning
  • Artwork and branding method
  • Label and packaging needs
  • Delivery country and timeline
  • Quality control expectations

Good production is not just manufacturing. It is planning, approvals, quality checks and delivery working together.

Bring your production brief to TShirtSwiss and we will help map the right route from sample to final shipment.

Quality and logistics

Finished goods should be checked, packed and prepared for the destination.

Production does not end when garments are sewn or decorated. Before dispatch, products may need measurement checks, branding checks, packaging checks, size sorting, carton labels and shipping preparation. These details matter for buyers who need products ready for retail, teams, events, stores or international distribution.

TShirtSwiss can help define quality control and packing expectations before production begins so the final goods are easier to receive, inspect and distribute.

Production FAQ

Can you help before I have a final spec?

Yes. Product development and sampling can help shape a product before bulk production begins.

Can production include printing or embroidery?

Yes. Screen printing, embroidery, sublimation and heat transfer can be selected depending on product, fabric, artwork and quantity.

Can you handle labels and packaging?

Yes. Custom labels, hang tags and packaging can be planned as part of the production route.

Can goods be shipped internationally?

Yes. Finished orders can be prepared for worldwide shipping from Thailand.
