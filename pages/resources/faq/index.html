Frequently asked questions about working with TShirtSwiss.

These answers are designed to help apparel buyers understand how to prepare for production. Every project is different, so the best route depends on the garment type, quantity, decoration method, sampling needs, delivery country and brand requirements.

Use this FAQ as a practical starting point, then contact us with your specifications so we can confirm the most suitable production plan.

Production and ordering

What products can TShirtSwiss manufacture?

We support custom t-shirts, polos, hoodies, workwear, corporate apparel, healthcare uniforms, sportswear, rashguards, fight shorts, caps, tote bags, promotional merchandise and private label clothing.

Do you support private label clothing?

Yes. We can support garment production, woven labels, printed labels, hang tags, packaging and quality control for private label apparel brands.

Can you help with samples?

Yes. Sampling can be planned before bulk production depending on the product, artwork, fabric and project requirements.

What information should I provide for a quote?

Share product type, quantity, sizes, colours, artwork, branding requirements, deadline, delivery country and whether you need sampling, labels or packaging.

Branding and decoration

Can garments include screen printing?

Yes. Screen printing can be used for many t-shirts, hoodies, tote bags and merchandise projects depending on fabric, artwork and quantity.

Can you provide embroidery?

Yes. Embroidery can be used for polos, caps, jackets, corporate apparel and selected uniform projects.

Do you offer sublimation?

Yes. Sublimation can be used for sportswear, rashguards, fightwear and other suitable garments where all-over artwork or performance graphics are required.

Can you help with packaging?

Yes. Packaging can include folding, polybags, size sorting, stickers, inserts, hang tags, carton organisation and location-based packing.

Quality control and shipping

How is quality controlled?

Quality control can include checks for garment construction, measurements, artwork placement, print or embroidery quality, labelling, packaging and carton organisation.

Can you ship internationally?

Yes. Finished orders can be prepared for worldwide shipping from Thailand. Delivery planning depends on destination, order size and required timeline.

Can orders be packed by size or location?

Yes. Orders can be organised by size, style, department, branch, team, kit or destination when packing requirements are provided in advance.

Can you support repeat orders?

Yes. Repeat orders are easier when the approved garment, artwork, label, packing method and quality requirements are documented from the first order.
