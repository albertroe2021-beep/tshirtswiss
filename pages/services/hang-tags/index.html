Custom hang tags built for apparel projects that need stronger retail, ecommerce and brand presentation.

Hang tags are a small detail that can have a large effect on how a garment is presented. They can communicate brand story, product name, collection information, size, price, barcode, care highlights, sustainability notes or QR codes. For private label apparel and retail-ready clothing, a hang tag helps the product feel complete and professional.

Why hang tag planning matters

A hang tag should be planned with the garment, label and packaging route. Paper stock, size, hole position, string type, barcode placement, price display, folding and packing method can all affect how the finished product is handled and presented. If tags are produced too late or without clear information, they can slow down final packing.

Best fit projects

Custom hang tags are especially useful for retail apparel, ecommerce products, private label clothing, creator merchandise, sportswear collections, uniform programs and any project where the garment needs a customer-facing product identity.

Hang tags in practice

A hang tag should connect product information, brand identity and final presentation in a clean, practical way.

Planning the hang tag

Good hang tags start with clear product information and a practical packing route.

The strongest hang tags balance brand storytelling with practical information. A retail tag may need barcode, price, size, material details and collection name. An ecommerce tag may focus more on brand message, QR code, care guidance or reorder information. The design should support the garment without making packing or fulfilment harder.

We review these details before production so tags are practical to attach and suitable for the finished garment. This keeps final presentation professional and helps avoid missing information or delays at the packing stage.

Hang tags decision guide

When custom hang tags are the right next step.

Custom hang tags are usually the right step when apparel needs to feel retail-ready, ecommerce-ready or clearly connected to a brand story. They are especially important when garments will be sold, gifted, stocked, distributed or presented to customers as branded products rather than generic apparel.

If the garment still needs internal brand identity, custom labels may come first. If the full order needs folding, polybags, stickers or carton organisation, hang tags should be planned together with packaging solutions. We review the project stage before recommending the right route.

Hang tags FAQ

What information can hang tags include?

Hang tags can include brand story, product name, barcode, price, size, material details, QR codes, care notes and collection information.

Can hang tags be added to private label garments?

Yes. Hang tags are often integrated into private label apparel together with custom labels and packaging.

Do I need finished artwork?

Finished artwork is helpful, but we can review the layout, dimensions and information requirements before production.

Can you attach tags to garments?

Yes. Tag application can be coordinated with garment production and final packing depending on the project.

Can hang tags be combined with packaging?

Yes. Hang tags, labels, stickers, polybags and carton packing can be planned together for a complete presentation.

Can you ship internationally?

Yes. Finished garments, tags and related production orders can be packed and shipped worldwide.
