Custom labels built for apparel projects that need a complete branded finish.

Custom clothing labels are one of the most important details in private label apparel because they turn a garment into a branded product. A label can communicate the brand name, size, care instructions, fibre content, origin, collection identity or retail presentation. When planned correctly, labels help the garment feel finished and commercially ready.

Why label planning matters

Labels need to be chosen with the garment, fabric and end use in mind. A woven hem label, printed neck label, satin care label and heat transfer size mark all create different results. Size, colour, readability, wash durability and placement should be confirmed before bulk production starts.

Best fit projects

Custom labels are especially useful for private label clothing, ecommerce apparel, retail collections, uniforms, sportswear, merchandise and any garment where brand presentation matters beyond the external print or embroidery.

Custom labels in practice

A label should feel like part of the garment, not an afterthought added at the end of production.

Planning the label

Good labels start with the right material, size and placement.

Different label types serve different purposes. A woven neck label can create a premium brand feel, a printed neck label can reduce irritation, a care label can carry required washing information and a small hem label can add subtle external branding. Choosing the right option depends on garment type, customer expectations and production requirements.

We review these details before production so labels are practical to apply and suitable for the finished garment. This keeps the branding professional and helps avoid uncomfortable, oversized or poorly positioned label details.

Custom labels decision guide

When custom labels are the right next step.

Custom labels are usually the right step when you want apparel to carry your own brand identity or when you need size, care and product information presented professionally. They are especially important for ecommerce, retail, private label and uniform projects where the garment should feel finished and repeatable.

If you are still developing the garment, labels can be planned alongside sampling. If the garment is already approved, labels can be added as part of OEM or private label production. We review the project stage before recommending the right label route.

Production management

Why professional label management makes a difference

Once a custom label project moves into production, the final result depends on many small details. Text legibility, colour contrast, stitch position, label softness, fold type, wash information and sizing systems all affect how the garment feels to the customer. If these details are not controlled, the garment can look unfinished or create avoidable customer confusion.

TShirtSwiss helps coordinate label requirements with the garment production route. We review the artwork, advise on practical limitations, confirm placement and support quality control before final packing. This helps ensure labels support the brand rather than creating problems during production or use.

Reducing risk for branded garment production

For international clients, visibility is important. You need confidence that labels are produced correctly and applied to the right garments in the right position. Our Swiss-managed approach keeps label production structured from artwork review through to final garment presentation.

Complete apparel solution

Building a complete apparel solution around custom labels

Most custom label projects are part of a wider apparel production plan. A brand may need neck labels, care labels, size labels, hang tags and packaging for a launch order. A uniform program may need consistent size and care information across multiple garment styles. An ecommerce brand may need labels and packaging that support repeat ordering and customer fulfilment.

Looking at labels in isolation can create timing issues or presentation gaps. We prefer to understand the garment, brand, customer and delivery requirements before confirming label production. That helps connect labels with sampling, OEM production, private label manufacturing, hang tags, packaging, quality control and shipping.

Custom labels FAQ

What label types can you support?

Can labels be added to private label garments?

Yes. Custom labels are often integrated into private label apparel during sampling and bulk production.
