A controlled embroidery production process

Each stage is planned to keep logos consistent across the complete order.

1

Artwork review

Logo detail, scale and intended garment placement are reviewed before digitising.

2

Digitising

The artwork is converted into a stitch file with suitable density and direction.

3

Sampling

A sample confirms thread colour, stitch finish, position and garment stability.

4

Production and QC

Bulk embroidery is checked for placement, loose threads and presentation.

Planning the embroidery

Good embroidery starts with the right logo, garment and placement.

Logo size, stitch count, thread colour, fabric weight and garment construction all affect the final result. We review these factors together so the embroidery supports the garment rather than causing puckering, distortion or poor legibility.

  • Logo digitising review
  • Thread colour planning
  • Logo size and placement
  • Garment stability check
  • Sampling recommendation
  • Bulk production control
