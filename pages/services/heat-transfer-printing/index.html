Heat transfer printing for detail, flexibility and fast-moving apparel projects.

Heat transfer printing is a practical decoration method for apparel projects that need sharp graphics, sponsor logos, player names, numbers or variable artwork without committing to large screen-printing setups. It is especially useful for sportswear, work uniforms, event merchandise, retail apparel and garments where the artwork changes between pieces.

TShirtSwiss manages heat transfer production in Thailand with Swiss-style communication, practical garment guidance, sampling support and quality control. We help choose the right approach for your fabric, artwork, quantity and budget so the final garment feels professional and performs as intended.

Best use cases

Heat transfer is ideal for team kits, staff uniforms, sponsor marks, individual names, numbering, short-run merchandise and detailed logos that need a clean finish.

  • Sports names and numbers
  • Sponsor logos
  • Small production runs
  • Detailed artwork
  • Technical garments
  • Event apparel

Crisp print detail without overcomplicating production.

Heat transfer FAQ

When should I choose heat transfer?

Choose heat transfer for names, numbers, sponsor logos, small runs and detailed artwork where screen setup may not be practical.

Can it be used on sportswear?

Yes. We review the garment fabric, stretch and intended use before recommending the right transfer approach.

Can you combine it with other services?

Yes. Many projects combine embroidery, screen printing, labels or packaging with heat transfer placements.

Do you provide samples?

Sampling is recommended when artwork size, placement, feel or durability needs approval before bulk production.

Need heat transfer printing for your apparel project?

Send us the garment type, artwork, placements, quantities and delivery country. We will recommend the best print method and production route.
