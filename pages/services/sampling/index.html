Sampling built for apparel projects that need confidence before committing to bulk manufacturing.

Sampling is one of the most important stages in custom apparel production because it turns an idea, sketch, reference garment or tech pack into something that can be checked, worn and improved. A sample helps confirm whether the fabric feels right, whether the fit works, whether the construction is practical and whether the branding details match the intended product.

Why sampling matters

Without a clear sample approval process, bulk production can move forward with unresolved questions. Small issues in measurement, print placement, embroidery scale, label position, garment weight or sewing detail can become expensive once repeated across hundreds or thousands of pieces. Sampling helps resolve these issues before production volume increases.

Best fit projects

Sampling is especially valuable for new brands, ecommerce collections, sportswear projects, corporate uniforms, private label clothing, OEM manufacturing and any project where the garment must meet specific fit, fabric or branding expectations.

Sampling in practice

A sample should give you something clear to approve, adjust or reject before bulk manufacturing begins.

Planning the sample

Good sampling starts with a clear production goal.

A sample should be created to answer specific questions. Are we checking fit, fabric, branding, construction, colour, packaging or all of these together? The clearer the purpose of the sample, the easier it is to evaluate the result and provide useful feedback.

We review the sampling objective early so the garment is built around the right priorities. A fit sample may focus on measurement and construction, while a sales sample may need branding, labels, packaging and presentation closer to the final product.

Sampling decision guide

When sampling is the right next step.

Sampling is usually the right step when the product is new, the fit matters, the fabric has not been confirmed, the branding details need to be tested or the garment will be produced in meaningful quantity. It is also important when working with private label or OEM apparel where the sample becomes the reference for manufacturing.

Sampling may not be necessary for every simple reorder or very basic promotional item, but even straightforward projects benefit from a clear approval reference. We review the garment, timeline, budget and production risk before recommending the right sample route.

  • Best for new products
  • Strong for fit approval
  • Ideal before bulk production
  • Useful for branding checks
  • Supports private label projects
  • Can reduce production risk

Sampling FAQ

Why do I need a sample?

A sample helps confirm fit, fabric, construction, branding and finishing before committing to bulk production.

Can you create a sample from a reference garment?

Yes. Reference garments, sketches, tech packs and photos can help guide the sampling process.

Can the first sample be changed?

Yes. Feedback can be used to revise measurements, fabric, placement, trims or construction before approval.

Does sampling include labels and packaging?

It can. Sales and pre-production samples often include labels, branding and packaging details for approval.

Is a sample the same as bulk production?

No. A sample is a reference garment. Once approved, it helps guide the bulk production specification.

Can you ship samples internationally?

Yes. Samples and finished production orders can be shipped internationally.
