Franchise Apparel Programmes

Franchises Need Apparel Systems, Not One-Off Uniform Orders

A franchise apparel programme has to do more than produce branded clothing. It needs to keep multiple locations looking consistent, support new staff onboarding, make reorder planning easier and protect brand standards across different teams, seasons and franchise owners.

For restaurants, fitness studios, service businesses, retail concepts, hospitality brands, cleaning companies, wellness chains and field-service networks, the apparel system can include polos, t-shirts, aprons, caps, jackets, workwear, promotional merchandise, staff packs and opening kits. Each item should follow a clear specification so future locations can order the same garments with the same logo placement, colours, decoration method, labels and packaging logic.

TShirtSwiss.ch supports franchises with Swiss-managed production in Thailand, connecting product selection, sampling, embroidery, screen printing, heat transfer, labels, packaging, quality control and worldwide shipping. The goal is to create a repeatable apparel route that supports the brand as it grows.

Programme Planning

What Should Be Standardised for a Franchise?

Franchise apparel works best when the brand defines a clear standard for each garment. This can include the garment style, fabric, colour, size range, logo placement, embroidery size, print colours, name or role details, label requirements, packaging rules and carton organisation.

Standardisation makes it easier to onboard new locations and maintain a consistent brand image. It also reduces decision-making for franchise owners because the approved apparel range is already defined.

  • Approved garments and colours
  • Logo size and placement rules
  • Staff role and department differences
  • Opening kit and reorder quantities
  • Packaging by branch or location
  • Quality control before dispatch

Franchise FAQs

Common Questions About Franchise Uniforms

Can you support multiple franchise locations?

Yes. Apparel can be planned by location, branch, staff role, size set or opening kit requirements.

Can the same uniform be reordered later?

Yes. The goal is to document garment specifications, branding and packaging standards so repeat orders are easier.

What garments work best for franchises?

Polos, t-shirts, aprons, caps, jackets, hoodies, workwear and promotional merchandise are common options.

Can orders be packed by branch?

Yes. Packaging can be planned by branch, department, role, staff group, size range or launch kit.
