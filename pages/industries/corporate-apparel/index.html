Corporate apparel buying guide

Choosing apparel for company teams.

Corporate buyers should consider more than colour and logo placement. The right garment depends on the department, climate, staff role, washing frequency, customer-facing expectations and the way garments will be distributed across employees.

For office and sales teams, fit and presentation may matter most. For field teams, durability and comfort often matter more. For onboarding kits and events, packaging, timing and brand experience can be just as important as the garment itself.

  • Prioritise staff comfort
  • Match the brand style
  • Plan role-based garments
  • Use consistent branding
  • Plan reorder cycles
  • Keep distribution simple
