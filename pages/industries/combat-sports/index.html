Fightwear buying guide

Choosing apparel for combat sports teams and brands.

Combat sports buyers should consider more than artwork and colour. The right garment depends on the discipline, movement, grip exposure, washing frequency, print method, team identity and whether the order is for athletes, members, retail customers or event participants.

For academies, durability and repeat ordering can be critical. For gyms, team identity and comfort often matter most. For fightwear brands, sampling, fit consistency, labels and packaging can be just as important as the garment itself.

  • Prioritise movement
  • Match fabric to discipline
  • Choose the right decoration
  • Plan sponsor artwork
  • Build brand identity
  • Keep reorders simple
