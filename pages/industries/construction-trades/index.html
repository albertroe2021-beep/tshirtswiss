Construction workwear buying guide

Choosing apparel for construction and trade teams.

Workwear buyers should consider more than colour and logo placement. The right garment depends on the trade, working environment, washing frequency, movement needs, climate, customer-facing expectations and the way garments will be distributed across staff.

For field crews, durability and comfort may matter most. For customer-facing service teams, logo placement, cleanliness and consistent colour can strengthen trust. For larger contractors, replacement cycles, size records and department packing can make uniform management easier.

  • Prioritise durability
  • Plan for movement
  • Consider climate and layering
  • Use consistent branding
  • Plan replacement cycles
  • Keep reorders simple
