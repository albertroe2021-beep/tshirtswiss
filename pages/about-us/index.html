About TShirtSwiss

TShirtSwiss connects international clients with structured apparel manufacturing in Thailand, managed with Swiss expectations for communication, quality, organisation and long-term reliability.

Built for brands and companies that want manufacturing support without guesswork.

TShirtSwiss was created to make apparel production in Thailand easier for international clients to plan, manage and repeat. We work with brands, corporate teams, event organisers, sports clubs, healthcare providers, ecommerce stores, franchise groups and private label businesses that need a production partner who can communicate clearly and keep the project organised.

Our role is to connect the commercial needs of the buyer with practical factory execution. That means helping clients define products, samples, materials, branding, labels, packaging, quality checks and shipping requirements before production begins.

Our approach

Swiss management, Thai production capability and international delivery.

Thailand offers strong garment manufacturing capability, skilled production teams and flexible apparel options. TShirtSwiss adds structure around that capability so international buyers can work through a clear process instead of managing every detail alone.

We focus on practical production planning, transparent communication and quality expectations that are defined before bulk production starts.

  • Clear project briefing
  • Sampling support
  • Branding and label planning
  • Quality control options
  • Packing and shipping support
  • Repeat order planning

Who we work with

Manufacturing support for companies, brands, teams and organisations.

Our clients come to us for different reasons. Some need custom uniforms, some need private label apparel, some need event merchandise, some need fightwear or sportswear, and others need help turning a product idea into a repeatable production system.

About TShirtSwiss FAQ

Where is production based?

Production is coordinated through Thailand, with Swiss-managed communication and project oversight for international clients.

Who do you work with?

We work with brands, businesses, franchises, teams, events, ecommerce stores, healthcare providers and organisations that need custom apparel production.

Can you support private label brands?

Yes. We support private label clothing with garments, labels, hang tags, packaging and quality control planning.

Can you help with product development?

Yes. Product development and sampling can be planned before bulk production depending on the garment and project requirements.

Ready to discuss your apparel project?

Send us your product idea, quantity, branding requirements and delivery country. We will help you understand the best production route.
